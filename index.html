Australia’s Internet Reliance

A visual story about where the NBN is ready, where it is actually being used, and why fixed-line broadband still carries the weight of Australia’s online life.

Audience: Everyday Australians comparing broadband access and performance.

Key Questions: Is NBN Reliable? Fast? Convenient? Accessible?

Why does access to the internet matter?

Internet access is now considered essential for study, work, entertainment and public services, as well as much, much more. The charts below combine national download-volume data, measured nbn plan speeds, and state-level network premises data to tell a simple story: Australia has built broad broadband reach, but the lived experience depends on take-up, technology mix and plan speed.

1

Access: Ready does not always mean Active!

While many NBN services are available around the country, not all of them are activated regardless of if they're ready to connect.

A sizeable number of premises have inactive connections to the NBN, waiting to be activated.

While fixed line connections are obviously important, other connection types such as wireless and satellite are incredibly important for non-eastern states with higher remote populations.

The larger the state, the more inactive connections waiting to be used. More population = more services.

2

Performance: Do NBN plans meet their advertised speeds?

Once connected, plan speed becomes the everyday experience. Measured speeds cluster close to plan labels, showing many plans meet their targets.

These lines are nearly perfectly straight because measured plan speeds have remained consistent between the years.

The pale bars show advertised plan labels; the coloured bars show the ACTUAL measured 2022 speed for each plan. The faster the plan, the bigger the relative gap. For the most part, average speeds match their advertised plan speeds.

3

Demand: Australians keep downloading more

Demand kept climbing: total downloads rose from 3.0 million TB to 3.8 million TB across one year. More and more people are using the internet every day.

Despite mobile and wireless growth, fixed line connections carried the overwhelming majority of download volume and continues to do so.

While wireless data usage has grown, it remains a small fraction of total data usage compared to fixed line.

Majority of Australians' used fixed line connections for downloads, likely due to the differences in speed and also reliability.
